Respectful Workplace and Grievance Procedure (Example)
Category: Conduct | Example edition: 2025

Raising a concern
Employees are encouraged to raise workplace concerns informally with their manager where appropriate. If the concern involves the manager, or informal discussion is unsuitable, the employee may contact a designated HR representative.

Formal grievance
An employee may submit a formal grievance in writing to HR, describing the concern, relevant dates, and the outcome sought. HR should acknowledge receipt within five working days and arrange a meeting where appropriate. Employees may bring a workplace colleague to a formal grievance meeting.

Review and response
The company should consider relevant information impartially and provide a written outcome. The employee may request a review by HR within ten working days of receiving the outcome, explaining the reasons for the request. This synthetic example does not define statutory rights or replace local legal requirements.
