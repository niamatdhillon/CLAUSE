CLAUSE SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE POLICY — NOT AN ACTUAL EMPLOYER POLICY
Leave and Time Away (Example)
Category: Leave | Example edition: 2025

Annual leave
For this fictional example only, full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time leave is prorated according to contracted hours. Unused leave treatment is subject to applicable law and the employee's written terms; this example does not define carryover.

Requesting leave
Employees should submit planned annual leave requests to their manager at least ten working days before the first day requested. The manager should consider team coverage and respond in the leave system. Short-notice requests should be discussed with the manager as soon as practical. A request is not approved until confirmed in the leave system.

Sickness absence
Employees who are unable to work due to illness should notify their manager as soon as practical on the first day of absence and follow any applicable local reporting requirements. This example does not specify sick pay or medical certification rules.
